Thank you to everyone who has shown TimeDuck such wonderful support and interest. I honestly did not expect this little vibe-coding project to come this far, and I’m grateful to everyone who has tried it, followed along, and shared encouragement. I’m looking forward to continuing the updates. Thanks again for being here.

TimeDuck 1.2.0 consolidates DuckDrops Waves 2 through 8.2 into one large update after v1.1.0.

## Highlights

- Living costumes, seasonal drops, secret moments, reduced-motion behavior, and accessibility labels.
- Three-tier duck interactions, feeding, Chonky mode, expanded phrases, and CRT startup presentation.
- Five procedural Pomodoro stories with supporting characters, animated props, transition choreography, and finales.
- MiniHUD safe-zone layout, five clock scales, compact story scenes, and sleeping effects.
- Five TimeCompanions, twenty achievements, CRT toasts, and the three-tab Duckbook journal.
- Offline AI LiveSplit protocol with loopback HTTP, `timeduck://` controls, labeled laps, terminal helpers, and GPU timing detection.
- Version-aware What's New panel with a release spotlight and Wave 9 horizon preview.

## Evidence

- `./build.sh --test`: 262 passed, 0 failed.
- `./build.sh --release`: release application bundle built successfully.

## Scope note

Wave 9's world clock, scheduled alarms, digital menu-bar clock, and ambient chimes remain a separate design track. See [`docs/WAVE_9_HANDOFF.md`](https://github.com/VinceGuyMan/TimeDuck/blob/main/docs/WAVE_9_HANDOFF.md).
