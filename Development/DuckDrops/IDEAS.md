# 💡 TimeDuck Future Ideas & Feature Backlog

A curated collection of future ideas, community suggestions, and experimental companion mechanics.

---

## 🦆 Companion Antics & Micro-Interactions

- **Duck Bread Crumb Physics**: Tiny interactive bread crumbs dropped via mouse click with realistic flocking waddle.
- **Weather / Night Shifts**: Subtle night-sky stars or raindrops during late-night focus sessions.
- **Duck Nap Dreams**: Tiny thought bubbles displaying 8-bit bread, crowns, or timer clocks during deep sleep.
- **Companion Sound Effects**: Subtle distinct quack sound variations for specific hats (e.g. 8-bit synthesized quack for Cyber Shades, regal brass fanfare quack for Crown).

## ⚙️ Core Platform & Workflow Options (Only if Requested)

- **Configurable Global Hotkeys**: Quick toggle for start/pause from background apps.
- **Menu-Bar-Only Mode**: Option to hide dock icon and run entirely as an animated status item.
- **Custom Sound Importer**: Ability to drop a local `.wav` or `.m4a` file into `Application Support/TimeDuck/Audio/`.
