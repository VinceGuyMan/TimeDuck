## 📋 Detailed Concepts Breakdown

### Concept 01: Arcade Phosphor (The Retro Terminal)
- **Visual Description**: Deep obsidian arcade housing with an inner CRT monitor bezel, phosphor scanlines, glowing `> 25:00` digits, and our canonical neutral TimeDuck centered in high-contrast neon green aura.
- **Atmosphere**: Pure 80s arcade monitor / VT220 hacker terminal.
- **Best For**: Direct nod to TimeDuck's CRT retro roots.
