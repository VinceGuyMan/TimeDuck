# TimeDuck Canonical Font Specification
=======================================

The official **TimeDuck** custom font (`TimeDuck.ttf`) is the single authoritative typeface for the TimeDuck desktop instrument and companion ecosystem.

---

## 🔤 Font File Information

- **Font Name**: `TimeDuck`
- **Family**: `TimeDuck`
- **Style**: `Regular` (Unified Master)
- **File**: [`TimeDuck.ttf`](file:///Users/homebase/Documents/TimeDuck-DuckDrops/TimeDuck-Canonical-Art/v1/fonts/TimeDuck.ttf)
- **Units per EM**: `2048`

---

## 🧬 Architectural Lineage & Composition

The `TimeDuck` custom font is remixed directly from our source typography:
1. **Uppercase Letters (A-Z)**: Tactical Metal Gear Solid Codec typography — bold, condensed, and military-precise for heroic headlines, UI, and menus.
2. **Numerals (0-9)**: High-tech radar and telemetry countdown digits tuned specifically for `> 25:00`, stopwatch readouts, and lap counters.
3. **Punctuation & Prompts**: Signature prompt `>` and countdown colon `:`.
4. **Embedded Mascot Glyph**: Native 13×10 TimeDuck companion vector glyph embedded at `§` (`U+00A7`) and `¤` (`U+00A4`).
